Three Core Bottlenecks in Standard Federated Learning
1. Disjoint Label Spaces

11 non-overlapping diagnoses across 3 modalities cannot share a single classifier head.

2. Sample-Size Bias

Size-weighted FedAvg gives Site B only 2.7% weight while Site C dictates 73.4%.

3. Uncalibrated Confidence

Raw softmax outputs are overconfident; clinical safety requires calibrated referral.

Proposition 1 (Referral Lower Bound)
Referral Rate ≥ | c − a |

Where c is coverage and a is top-1 accuracy (if sets are non-empty, c ≥ a). Demanding 95% coverage on an 87% accurate ultrasound model forces ~50% referrals—motivating site-specific targets.

Clinical Insight: High conformal coverage alone does not imply high automation; coverage targets must be matched to each hospital's underlying accuracy.

Conclusion

Summary and Key Contributions

1. Decoupled Architecture

Shared EfficientNetV2-S + CBAM with local BatchNorm and heads supports disjoint modalities and label spaces.

2. Weighting Resilience

Equal weighting + local fine-tuning reach 93.30% mean accuracy and prevent a 30% collapse on scarce ultrasound.

3. Calibrated Clinical Triage

Temperature scaling cuts ECE by 39%, Proposition 1 bounds referral via |c − a|, and LAC hits 98%+ singleton accuracy.

Practical Guideline for Clinical Deployment: Combine equal client weighting with per-site calibration and site-specific conformal coverage targets to protect small hospitals without overwhelming radiologists.
